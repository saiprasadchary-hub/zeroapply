CATEGORY 01

General & Product Questions

Understanding what ZeroApply does and how it fits into your daily job search.

What is ZeroApply and why was it created?+

ZeroApply is a native desktop application designed to eliminate the repetitive manual friction of job applications. Modern job portals require applicants to manually re-type work history, education, certifications, and links over and over again. ZeroApply acts as your local single source of truth, letting you copy structured background information with a single click and organize tailored resumes and pipeline statuses in one focused desktop workspace.

How is ZeroApply different from a browser extension?+

Browser extensions often inject heavy scripts into every page you visit, require broad browser permissions to read your web activity, and can break when job sites update their frontend code. ZeroApply is a standalone native desktop app. It sits cleanly alongside your browser, does not access your browsing history, and works across every website, email client, and portal without permission risks.

Can I use ZeroApply without an internet connection?+

Yes. ZeroApply is built offline-first. Your career profile, resume variations, and pipeline tracking database live entirely on your computer's local hard drive. You can review and edit your application materials while traveling or completely offline.

Can I store multiple versions of my resume?+

Yes. The Resume Studio allows you to create as many tailored resume versions as you need—for example, a "Lead Frontend Engineer" variant highlighting design systems, and a "Full-Stack Engineer" variant emphasizing backend APIs. You can organize distinct bullet points for each variation.

CATEGORY 02

Privacy & Data Security

How your sensitive employment information is protected on your device.

Where is my personal data stored?+

All data is saved in a local database file in your Windows user profile folder (%APPDATA%\ZeroApply\). ZeroApply does not upload your work history, salary expectations, resumes, or application records to any remote server or cloud bucket.

Does ZeroApply sell or share data with recruiters or third parties?+

Never. ZeroApply has no telemetry trackers, no analytics SDKs, and no advertiser partnerships. We believe your career search is personal, and your data belongs exclusively to you.

Does ZeroApply automatically submit applications without my review?+

No. ZeroApply is designed to assist your workflow, not replace you. You remain in total control of every single application. You review, approve, and submit all portals yourself to ensure 100% accuracy and customized attention for each company.

How do I backup or export my data?+

Because ZeroApply uses standard local files, you can back up your entire profile and pipeline simply by copying your workspace.db file from %APPDATA%\ZeroApply\ to a USB drive or secure backup folder.

CATEGORY 03

Platforms, Installation & Pricing

Information about supported operating systems, updates, and licensing.

Which operating systems are supported?+

The current desktop package targets 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11. It installs as a lightweight desktop application with zero heavy background services.

When will macOS and Linux builds be released?+

Builds for macOS (Apple Silicon & Intel) and Linux (.AppImage / .deb) are actively in development. You can check back on this website or monitor our release channels for build announcements.

Is ZeroApply really free, or is there a paid subscription?+

ZeroApply is free desktop software. There are no monthly subscription fees, no locked features, and no credit card requirements.

How do I uninstall ZeroApply if I land a job?+

You can uninstall ZeroApply anytime via Windows Settings > Installed Apps > ZeroApply > Uninstall. The uninstaller removes all application files cleanly from your system.
