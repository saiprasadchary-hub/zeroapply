FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is ZeroApply?+

ZeroApply is a native desktop application designed to organize your job search. It centralizes your career profile, resume variations, and application tracking in one fast, private workspace so you never have to re-type repetitive details across job sites.

Which platforms are supported?+

The current desktop package targets Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). macOS and Linux builds are in development and will be released when testing is complete.

Is my personal data private?+

Yes, completely. ZeroApply runs locally on your PC. Your resumes, profile details, and job submissions are stored on your local drive and are never uploaded to any remote analytics or third-party servers.

Am I always in control of my applications?+

Yes. ZeroApply does not submit applications automatically without your explicit action. You review, approve, and finalize every application yourself with total transparency.
